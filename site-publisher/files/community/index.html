More than putting on shows

BACKING THE PEOPLE WHO MAKE COMEDY HAPPEN.

Great comedy nights don't happen by accident. They need performers, organisers, venues and audiences who care about the scene. Here are some of the ways RockCentral supports the people behind live comedy.

Our commitment to comedy

WHAT WE DO.

Six practical ways we support comics and others working in live comedy.

01 / ACCESS

FREE TICKETS FOR COMEDY PEOPLE

Comics and anyone connected to the live comedy industry can request complimentary tickets to any RockCentral show, provided it isn't already sold out. Come and enjoy a show, support other acts or see a room in action.

Ask about tickets →
02 / OPPORTUNITIES

ROCKCENTRAL GIG SPOTS

Our private Facebook group, RockCentral Gig Spots, helps comics hear about gig opportunities and stay connected with what's happening at RockCentral.

Ask to join the group →
03 / DEVELOPMENT

COURSES AND WORKSHOPS

We run regular comedy courses and workshops for aspiring comedians. Whether you're preparing to try stand-up for the first time or looking to develop what you do on stage, we want to help more people give comedy a go.

04 / SAFETY

SAFER GIGS FOR EVERYONE

We work with Get Off! Live Comedy to support safer working environments in stand-up. Our own Code of Conduct also sets out expected behaviour and how to raise concerns.

05 / FAIR TREATMENT

PAID WITHIN 48 HOURS

Performers shouldn't have to chase their fees. We pay our acts within 48 hours of receiving their invoices.

06 / NEW TALENT

PAID RISING STAR SPOTS

Many of our professional bills include paid Rising Star open spots. It's a chance for newer comics to perform in front of a paying audience alongside established acts, with a fee for their work.

Respect on and off stage

COMEDY SHOULD BE GOOD TO ITS PEOPLE.

We want RockCentral shows to be places where audiences enjoy themselves, performers are treated fairly and newer acts get genuine opportunities.

That means setting clear expectations, being approachable when something goes wrong and taking the wider comedy community seriously.

Learn more about Get Off! Live Comedy and read our Code of Conduct.
