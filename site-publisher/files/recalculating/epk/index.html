Two people. Two stories. Plenty of wrong turns. Blending stand-up, storytelling and theatre, RECALCULATING sees Dawn Bailey and Anthony Williams explore the moment when the life you thought you wanted no longer feels like your own. For Dawn, that means leaving behind the life she was expected to live. Long synopsis · for brochures, programmes and websites

THE FULL STORY.

Two people. Two stories. One aim: to start again rather than settle for the lives they were always expected to live. Blending comedy, storytelling and theatre, RECALCULATING explores what happens when life in your forties stops going the way you thought it would. Dawn had built the kind of life she was meant to want: marriage, family routines and the full netball-mum schedule. Then she realised the destination no longer felt like hers, took an unexpected turn and started figuring out who she actually wanted to be. Anthony took a far less orderly route. His attempt to start over involved questionable decisions, wrong turns and a chain of unlikely events that culminated in what can only be described as a near-assassination attempt. Told from two very different perspectives, this is a funny, honest show about reinvention, risk, identity and finding the courage to change direction when the life ahead no longer feels like your own.

Appendix A · Technical rider

KEEP IT SIMPLE.

A deliberately lightweight, adaptable setup. Please confirm the specifics with us before the date; these requirements describe the full show, not a fixed design imposed on every room.

Essential

SOUND & MICROPHONES.

  • PA appropriate for the room and clear speech reproduction.
  • Three microphones in total: two onstage and a third offstage.
  • Two straight microphone stands for the onstage microphones.
  • A separate audio playback input into the PA or mixing desk.
  • Headset microphones are not required.
Flexible

LIGHTING.

  • An even warm-white front wash illuminating the playing area is sufficient.
  • Where available, an individual spotlight or focused area for the current performer is preferred.
  • Simple entrance / exit and blackout or fade cues are welcome, but complex lighting programming is not essential.
  • The company can discuss supplying basic lighting for less-equipped venues.
Two workable options

MUSIC & CUE PLAYBACK.

  • Venue-operated: we can provide audio files and a cue sheet for a technician to run.
  • Self-operated: the performers can trigger cues from their own device using a reliable Bluetooth receiver connected to the venue system.
  • A wired audio input is a sensible fallback if Bluetooth is unavailable or unstable.
  • All playback levels and connections should be tested at soundcheck.
Essential

STAGE & GET-IN.

  • Clear, safe playing area for two performers, two mic stands and one chair for Dawn.
  • No physical set, scenery or complicated props required.
  • Allow 30–60 minutes for get-in and soundcheck, depending on facilities.
  • Access to a standard UK mains socket near the playback position where needed.
  • No projector is required. Projection, if available, is optional.
Technical advance

OPERATION & PRACTICALITIES.

  • The production can self-operate where necessary; if the venue has a technician, we'll agree a simple cue sheet and responsibilities in advance.
  • We can discuss supplying PA and basic lights where the venue doesn't have suitable equipment. This must be agreed before the show.
  • Please confirm staging dimensions, mic provision, speaker coverage, lighting control, playback connection, soundcheck access, audience accessibility and the person responsible for operating cues.
  • RockCentral Promotions' existing production pack records £2m public liability insurance. Current documentation can be provided or reconfirmed for the booking.

THE ONE-MINUTE VENUE CHECK.

Can the audience clearly hear two onstage mics? Is there a third mic offstage? Is the playing area evenly lit? Can music cues be operated by your technician or from a performer-controlled device? Is there a chair and enough clear stage space? If yes, we can almost certainly make the show work. We'll confirm the details together.
