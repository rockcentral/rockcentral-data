About the show

THE SCENIC ROUTE.

At some point, the life you planned and the life you're living stop looking quite the same. Sometimes you follow the route. Sometimes you turn off, start again and hope the sat-nav knows what it's doing.

RECALCULATING brings two voices and two stories together in one comedy show about the unexpected directions life can take.
