About Richard

Richard Morton is an award-winning Geordie comedian, musician and songwriter, full of surprises and manic energy. His self-deprecating humour and razor-sharp observations find the funny in everyday life, often through hilarious original songs.

Richard has supported Jack Dee, Lee Evans, Jo Brand, Lily Savage and Phill Jupitus on tour, as well as appearing on television and radio. He is a winner of the Time Out Comedy Award.
