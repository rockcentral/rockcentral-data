The story so far

A proper all-rounder.

Richard Morton has never been easy to put in just one box. Stand-up, music, radio, television, songwriting. He has always found room for another idea.

Part of the British alternative comedy scene from its formative years, Richard was a founder member of The Comedy Store's Cutting Edge and won a Time Out Comedy Award in 1992.

His live career has taken him from Edinburgh and Montreal to major UK theatre tours alongside Jack Dee, Jo Brand, Lee Evans and Phill Jupitus. His television work includes The Comedy Store, Never Mind the Buzzcocks and Our Friends in the North, while his radio credits run from Just a Minute to documentaries for BBC Radio 4.
