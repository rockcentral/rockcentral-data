RockCentral Promotions · For venues & event organisers

BRING LIVE COMEDY TO YOUR VENUE.

We book the comedians, organise the show and provide PA and lighting where needed. One-off nights and regular clubs for pubs, breweries, arts centres and community venues.

LET'S TALK ↗WHAT WE HANDLE ↓

WHAT WE HANDLE

We agree the format, budget and responsibilities with you before booking. We can run the comedy side of the night or work alongside your existing team.

THE LINE-UP

Professional comedians, rising talent and experienced MCs chosen for your audience and the atmosphere you want.

THE SHOW

Running order, performer communication and confident management of the event from soundcheck to final applause.

THE TECH

Microphones, PA, lighting and stage requirements. We can bring equipment where needed and agree what's supplied in advance.

THE PROMOTION

Line-up information, performer images, event copy, and help with ticketing and promotion as agreed for your show.

HOW IT WORKS

01

TELL US ABOUT THE SPACE

Where you are, how many people you can seat, the facilities you have and the sort of night you'd like to put on.

02

WE PLAN THE RIGHT SHOW

We'll discuss format, performers, production, promotion, likely costs and who's handling what.

03

WE MAKE IT HAPPEN

Once we've agreed a plan, we book the line-up and run the comedy side of the event so you can focus on your venue and guests.
