Your next steps

GET YOUR SHOW READY.

Four things to sort now, followed by more festival information as it becomes available.

01 / CHECK YOUR LISTING

MAKE SURE YOUR SHOW APPEARS.

Find your show on the official festival box office. Check the show title, description, image, date, start time and ticket link.

If it is missing, please check that registration, payment, image and ticket-price setup have been completed on TixEvery. Let us know if anything needs correcting.

Festival box office →TixEvery →
02 / PROMOTE YOUR SHOW

START SPREADING THE WORD.

Post your direct ticket link, share with mailing lists, tell friends and get your show in front of audiences. You can use the festival's “I Have A Show” graphic while your own artwork is being prepared.

Tag @indiecomedyfest on Instagram, Independent Comedy Festival on Facebook, and RockCentral Promotions so we can share your posts.

Posters / optional printing

A3 POSTERS. SIMPLE AND AFFORDABLE.

You're welcome to print your own posters anywhere you like. We have also negotiated a price with our regular printer by combining multiple different show designs into one bigger print job.

£4

For three full-colour A3 posters

250gsm gloss paper. The price covers three posters of your own show design, not three different designs.

This is entirely optional. If you use your own printer, there's no charge from us for printing. We'll confirm payment and poster collection/distribution arrangements with anyone joining the group print run.

POSTER CHECKLIST.

  • Size: A3 portrait, 297 × 420mm.
  • Branding: the official Independent Comedy Festival logo is required. The RockCentral Promotions logo is optional.
  • Information: include your show title, date, time and the venue name, Real Ale Classroom, Leicester.
  • Print file: supply a print-ready PDF at A3 portrait size, with images suitable for printing.
  • File name: name your PDF after your show so we can match it to the right performer.
Upload your poster PDF →
Deadline: Saturday 23 January 2027. Please also email us to confirm you'd like to be included in the £4 print run.
