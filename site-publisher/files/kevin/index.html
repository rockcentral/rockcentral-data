About Kevin

Born in Glasgow, Kevin moved to England in 2000 and started performing stand-up in 2019. He has appeared at clubs including The Frog and Bucket, The Stand, Glee Club and Just the Tonic, and taken shows to comedy festivals around the UK.

He won the Silver Stand-Up competition at Leicester Comedy Festival in 2024, after reaching the British Comedian of the Year semi-finals in both 2022 and 2023.
