About Kevin

Kevin Daniel is an award-winning Scottish stand-up comedian based in the Midlands. Born in Glasgow, he moved to England in 2000 and first took to the stage as a stand-up in 2019.

He progressed from open spots and gong shows to regular paid work at comedy clubs across the UK. His credits include The Frog and Bucket, Hot Water, The Stand, Glee Club, Just the Tonic, Blackpool Comedy Station, Buzz Comedy, Comedy Hot Spots, The Hull Comedy Lounge and Funhouse Comedy, among many others.

Kevin's comedy brings together relatable stories, assured stagecraft and carefully crafted jokes. He has taken his own shows to the Edinburgh Fringe, Leicester Comedy Festival, Glasgow International Comedy Festival, Brighton Comedy Festival and Cambridge Fringe Festival.

He has also supported Jack Gleadow, Nathan Caton and Fiona Allen on their UK tours. In 2024 he won Leicester Comedy Festival's Silver Stand-Up competition.

Career highlights

  1. 2024

    Silver Stand-Up champion

    Won the Leicester Comedy Festival competition and performed his first full-hour show, What's the Worst That Can Happen?, to two sold-out audiences at the festival.

  2. 2023

    First solo show and growing recognition

    Sold-out work-in-progress performances of Freedom at Leicester and Glasgow comedy festivals. Supported Fiona Allen, reached the British Comedian of the Year semi-finals and featured in Chortle's Ones to Watch list.

  3. 2022

    British Comedian of the Year semi-finalist

    Reached the competition's semi-finals and supported Nathan Caton on tour.

  4. 2021

    Breakthrough recognition

    Finalist for Midlands Comedy Awards Breakthrough Act and tour support for Jack Gleadow.

  5. 2019

    First steps on stage

    Started performing stand-up and became a Midlands Comedy Awards Best New Act finalist.
