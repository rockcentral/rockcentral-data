Privacy policy

Drafted 9 November 2026

This page explains how information may be handled when you visit Kevin Daniel's comedy website or contact him about performances, press or other enquiries.

Pre-launch draft: The site operator needs to verify the final hosting, retention periods, third-party services and contact details before this notice is used for a public launch.

Who is responsible for your information?

For privacy enquiries relating to this website, contact Kevin Daniel at info@kevindanielcomedy.co.uk.

What information may be collected?

  • Contact details and messages: If you email Kevin, the information you send can include your name, email address, message content and any attachments.
  • Technical website data: The hosting provider may process IP addresses, browser and device information, dates, times and page requests in server or security logs.
  • Third-party content: Your browser may share technical information with outside services when it loads fonts, photographs or embedded videos, or when you follow an external link.

The current preview website has no on-site contact form, newsletter subscription or ticket checkout. Bookings are made through third-party ticket providers.

How is the information used?

Messages are used to respond to enquiries, discuss bookings and deal with professional correspondence. Depending on the circumstances, this processing may be necessary to take steps towards a contract or for legitimate interests in managing enquiries and performances.

Technical data may be processed to keep the website available, resolve faults and protect against misuse. The appropriate lawful basis for such processing is generally legitimate interests in maintaining a functional and secure website.

Other websites and services

This website currently uses fonts delivered by Google Fonts and videos embedded from YouTube using YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain. Your browser may connect to those services and transmit technical information, such as your IP address. Privacy-enhanced video embedding does not eliminate all data sharing. Google's information is at Google Privacy Policy.

Some photographs and other material on the preview website currently load from third-party websites, including Kevin's existing website. External sites may receive technical requests when those images load.

Ticketing websites, social networks and other linked services are independently operated. Their own privacy information applies when you visit them. This website does not itself collect payment-card information for those bookings.

Cookies and similar technologies

The current preview does not contain a first-party analytics or advertising tracking script. Embedded media and third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies. The site's use of these services, and whether consent controls are required, must be reviewed before launch.

Sharing and keeping your information

Information may be processed by providers used to operate the website and communications, including website hosting, email and embedded content services. Some providers may process information outside the UK. Any applicable safeguards depend on the provider and service.

Enquiry correspondence should only be kept for as long as necessary for the enquiry and any required follow-up. Exact email and server-log retention periods have not yet been confirmed for this new site and will be added before launch.

Your rights

Subject to the circumstances and any legal exemptions, UK data protection law may give you rights to access, correct or erase personal information; restrict or object to its processing; receive certain information in a portable format; and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a request or raise a concern, email info@kevindanielcomedy.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office if you are unhappy with how your personal information has been handled.

Changes to this policy

This notice will be reviewed when the new domain launches and when new features, such as a mailing list, contact form or analytics, are introduced. The date at the top of this page will be updated when the policy changes.
