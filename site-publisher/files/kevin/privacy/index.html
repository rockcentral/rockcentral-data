Privacy policy

New website policy draft | 9 November 2026

Knock Em Bandi Entertainment Ltd, trading as Kevin Daniel Comedy, is a UK-based company providing comedy entertainment by Kevin Daniel. This policy describes how information is handled when you use Kevin's website or contact the company.

For review before launch: This policy has been adapted from the existing Kevin Daniel Comedy website. We still need to confirm the new hosting provider, log and email retention periods, and whether any analytics or consent tools will be used.

Personal information we receive

Email enquiries. If you contact us, we receive the information you send, which may include your name, email address, message, booking details and attachments. We use it to respond to your enquiry, arrange performances or provide customer service. As stated in the previous site's policy, contact enquiries are not used for unrelated marketing.

Technical information. The hosting provider may handle IP addresses, browser and device information, dates and page requests as part of running and securing the website. The new hosting provider and its log-retention arrangements need to be confirmed.

Features not presently in use. The replacement site currently has no customer registration, comments, contact form, WooCommerce shop or on-site ticket checkout. It also does not currently include Google Analytics. The previous policy covered these functions, but we will only restore those disclosures if these features are introduced.

Why we use your information

We use correspondence to respond to you, discuss bookings, manage business relationships and provide support. Depending on the context, this may be necessary to take steps towards a contract or for our legitimate interests in operating the comedy business. Website technical data may be used for legitimate interests in security, reliability and fault resolution. We will seek consent where the law requires it.

Embedded videos and other external services

The new site embeds YouTube videos using YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may share technical information, such as your IP address, when connecting to these services. YouTube and Google have their own data and cookie practices. See Google's privacy policy.

Some photography on the preview site is currently loaded from Kevin's previous website or other sources. We plan to move approved photographs to the new site's own folders, but external servers may receive requests while the preview is in use.

Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X and YouTube are linked using ordinary links. They are not embedded social-feed widgets. You connect to those platforms when you follow a link, and their own privacy policies then apply.

Ticket links take visitors to third-party event or ticketing providers. Any purchase information and payment details are handled by those providers, not by Kevin's new website. Their privacy policies apply when you use their services.

Cookies

The current static website does not intentionally set first-party advertising or analytics cookies. Third-party services, including embedded videos, may use cookies or similar technology. We will review the finished site and any consent requirements before launch. You can also control cookies in your browser settings.

The previous site's policy listed WordPress authentication cookies, PHP session cookies and Google Analytics. Those descriptions have not been carried forward as current features because the replacement website does not presently use those systems.

Who can access information?

Authorised people working on behalf of Kevin Daniel Comedy and relevant email, hosting or technical providers may access information where needed to process enquiries, maintain the site or provide support. We do not share enquiry details for unrelated marketing. Personal information may also be disclosed where legally required.

The original policy identified Redcentaur as the previous hosting and support provider. The new site's hosting arrangements have not yet been confirmed, so we cannot accurately name its final provider or international transfer safeguards at this stage.

How long we keep information

We keep correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve enquiries, manage bookings and meet applicable legal requirements. The specific retention periods for email records and server or security logs must be checked and added before launch.

Keeping information secure

The website uses HTTPS to protect information sent between a visitor's browser and the web server in transit. Access to email and website administration should be restricted to authorised people. No system can guarantee absolute security, and final operational safeguards will be reviewed before launch.

Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances and any legal exemptions, you may have rights under UK data protection law to access, correct or erase your personal data, restrict or object to processing, receive certain information in a portable form, or withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis.

To make a request or raise a concern, contact info@kevindanielcomedy.co.uk. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Other websites and legal disclosures

Links from this site may lead to websites operated independently of Kevin Daniel Comedy. Please review their privacy notices before giving them information. We may respond to lawful requests for information from courts or public authorities, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Changes to this policy

We will review this notice when the new domain, kevindaniel.co.uk, launches and whenever website features, suppliers or the handling of personal information changes. We will update the policy date when revisions are made.
