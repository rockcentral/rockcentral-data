Biography.

Short biography

Kevin Daniel is an award-winning Scottish stand-up comedian based in the Midlands. Born in Glasgow, he began performing stand-up in 2019 and won Leicester Comedy Festival's Silver Stand-Up Competition in 2024. He performs at clubs and festivals across the UK.

Full biography

Kevin Daniel is an award-winning Scottish stand-up comedian based in the Midlands. Born in Glasgow, he moved to England in 2000 and started performing stand-up in 2019. He has appeared at The Frog and Bucket, The Stand, Glee Club, Just the Tonic and Funhouse Comedy, as well as comedy festivals around the UK. He has supported Fiona Allen, Nathan Caton and Jack Gleadow on tour, reached the British Comedian of the Year semi-finals in 2022 and 2023, and won Leicester Comedy Festival's Silver Stand-Up Competition in 2024. His stand-up combines assured storytelling and sharp observations.
