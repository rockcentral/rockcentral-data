About

About Anthony

Anthony Williams is a Derbyshire-based stand-up comedian and professional MC who has been performing since 2019. His style mixes warm audience interaction with polished material and a relaxed, confident stage presence.

He works regularly as an MC, opener, middle and closer, including paid work with Funhouse Comedy, Gag n Bone Man, Hilarity Bites, Jokes on Us, PIE Entertainment and Plane Comedy. He also produces and hosts professional comedy nights through RockCentral Promotions.

Anthony is proud to be represented by Mustard Talent Agency.
