The visiting variety experience

An old-fashioned club afternoon, with a few surprises.

Imagine the warmth of a proper working men's club variety afternoon, with live entertainment, familiar traditions, larger-than-life personalities and plenty of laughter. Think The Wheeltappers and Shunters Social Club with a little Phoenix Nights mischief.

There's comedy, music, audience involvement and, inevitably, some club business. We'll leave the finer details unexplained. It's more fun that way.

Straightforward arrangements

Two ways to welcome the club.

Option one

Book the show

Your venue books the experience for an agreed fee, sells the tickets and keeps the ticket income. You also benefit from your own bar and catering sales.

A good fit when your club already sells tickets and promotes its own events.

Option two

Let us promote it

RockCentral Promotions can manage ticket sales and promotion, with arrangements agreed to suit the venue and the likely audience. Your club can benefit from the extra visitors and bar trade.

Any venue share or fundraising contribution would be discussed and agreed in advance.

We can also explore a fundraising event where the figures work for everyone involved. There's no fixed price or one-size-fits-all package; we'll discuss the room, numbers and likely ticket demand first.

Behind the scenes

What we look after.

  • The entertainers and show running order
  • Production and experienced show management
  • PA, microphones and lighting where needed
  • Promotional materials and ticketing, as agreed

Produced by RockCentral Promotions, the team behind regular live comedy events across the region.

Your part

What we'd need from you.

  • A suitable room with seated audience space
  • Some possible dates, ideally for an afternoon show
  • Access to the room and any facilities we'll agree
  • A hand getting the word out locally

Food is optional. If your venue serves food, we can explore a buffet, sandwiches or a ticket that includes something to eat. No catering? That's fine too.

Applications for temporary premises

Have you got a room we could borrow?

Tell us your venue name, town, approximate seated capacity and any dates you have in mind. Let us know if you offer food or have a fundraising goal. We can work out the practical and financial details together.
