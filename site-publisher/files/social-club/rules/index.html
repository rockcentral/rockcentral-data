For the guidance of members

Club Rules & Bye-laws

The committee has issued a revised edition following a regrettable number of incidents which members will know perfectly well about.

The Secretary's revised edition · 12 sections · 36 clauses

1Conduct and general standards

  1. 1.1

    Members are asked to treat fellow members, staff and visiting turns with courtesy. There is no rule against enjoying yourself. This has been checked.

  2. 1.2

    The words “I’m only having a laugh” do not authorise the use of the chairman’s microphone.

  3. 1.3

    Members saying “you can’t say anything these days” may be asked to specify what they were planning to say.

2Seats and personal belongings

  1. 2.1

    Coats may not be used to reserve seats, including coats belonging to members who are not coming. The committee knows which coats these are.

  2. 2.2

    A handbag, half a pint and the words “he’s just popped out” do not entitle a member to retain six chairs.

  3. 2.3

    No member may claim the table beside the radiator by saying it was “their dad’s table”. Ownership of tables does not pass through families.

3Dress and admission

  1. 3.1

    Retro dress is encouraged but not compulsory. For anyone claiming to be in period costume, the committee recognises the period 1968–1983. Anything later is modern dress.

  2. 3.2

    The committee may describe a member as “a bit of a daft lad”. Following the February 2024 resolution, this is officially a gender-neutral term.

  3. 3.3

    Any member claiming to be “with the band” must identify which band and obtain confirmation from at least one member of it.

4Heating and ventilation

  1. 4.1

    The thermostat may be adjusted only by an authorised officer. Feeling cold does not confer authorisation.

  2. 4.2

    Members must not open all three windows to compensate for one radiator being switched on, even if they “just need a bit of air”.

  3. 4.3

    Under no circumstances is the radiator in the back lounge to be bled using the Treasurer’s teaspoon. The teaspoon has already been replaced twice.

5Food and refreshments

  1. 5.1

    Members may not bring mackerel-based food onto the premises without prior approval from the committee.

  2. 5.2

    Boiled eggs must not be peeled during bingo. The shells recovered from the ticket drum have been retained as evidence.

  3. 5.3

    The hand dryer is not to be used for warming pork pies. This applies to both sets of conveniences.

6Bingo and raffles

  1. 6.1

    Members calling “house” must be in possession of a completed ticket. A strong feeling is insufficient.

  2. 6.2

    The same tin of peaches must not be donated to the raffle for a fourth consecutive year. Its provenance is now known.

  3. 6.3

    A raffle prize may not be collected by the person who donated it simply because they “know what it’s worth”.

7Entertainment and visiting turns

  1. 7.1

    Members are asked not to sing the chorus before the entertainer reaches it. This is especially important during ballads.

  2. 7.2

    Calling every female vocalist “our Cilla” is discouraged, regardless of resemblance.

  3. 7.3

    No member may accompany a magician onto the stage uninvited to explain how the trick is done. Mr Hargreaves has been informed personally.

8Games and societies

  1. 8.1

    Snooker chalk must remain in the snooker room. It is not to be used to mark preferred places at the buffet.

  2. 8.2

    The dominoes committee accepts that vigorous play is traditional. It does not accept the need to shout “GET THAT DOWN YOU” after every double-six.

  3. 8.3

    Members must not refer to a disputed bowls result as “under police investigation” unless police have actually attended.

9Premises and equipment

  1. 9.1

    The emergency exit must remain unobstructed. “I was only putting the chairs there for a minute” is not an exemption.

  2. 9.2

    The lawnmower currently beneath the stage does not belong to the club. Whoever placed it there is asked to collect it without using the front entrance.

  3. 9.3

    No further attempt is to be made to reposition the trophy cabinet with four volunteers, a tea towel and the words “give it a shove”.

10Notices and correspondence

  1. 10.1

    Noticeboard dates must not be corrected in felt-tip pen merely because Tuesday would suit a member better.

  2. 10.2

    Anonymous complaints signed “Everybody” will be treated as submissions from one person.

  3. 10.3

    The club WhatsApp group is not a substitute for official minutes, particularly when messages are sent at 2.13am and consist only of photographs of a damp patch.

11Committee business

  1. 11.1

    Matters for Any Other Business must be submitted to the Secretary in advance. It is not an invitation to discuss everything that has annoyed a member since 1997.

  2. 11.2

    A statement beginning “I’m only saying” does not constitute a formal motion.

  3. 11.3

    Photographs of the committee must not be posted online with the caption “Crimewatch reconstruction”, even if the lighting is unflattering.

12Appeals, exceptions and final provisions

  1. 12.1

    Requests for exceptions must be made in writing. Saying “Mrs Entwistle said it would be all right” will be checked with Mrs Entwistle.

  2. 12.2

    Any appeal against a ruling must identify the rule concerned and explain why the member believes it should not apply. “Because Terry did it” is not an explanation.

  3. 12.3

    Applications to amend Rule 5.1 must specify the species, preparation method and intended room. “It’s only a little smoked one” will not be accepted.

A note from the Secretary

These rules are to be read in full. “I only read the ones that applied to me” will not be accepted as an excuse.

A note for visitors
