Ordinary meeting · 27 October 2026

Present: Mr Booth (Chair), Mrs Entwistle (Secretary), Mr Pilling (Treasurer), Mrs Sutcliffe (Entertainment). Apologies: Mr Hargreaves, via his wife.

  1. Minutes of the previous meeting. Approved, subject to replacing “heated exchange” with “full discussion”.
  2. Temporary entertainment arrangements. The committee agreed that taking the club to other venues would maintain morale. Mr Pilling asked whether morale would require a deposit.
  3. The incident. Mr Booth asked that this remain under item four. Mrs Entwistle noted that it was item three. Discussion deferred.
  4. Christmas draw. Mr Pilling asked whether the incident would affect the Christmas draw. The Chair stated that the incident had already affected quite enough.
  5. Any other business. No decision was reached on the thermostat.

Meeting closed at 9.14pm. Informal discussion continued in the car park.

Extraordinary meeting · 13 October 2026

The Chair welcomed members to the extraordinary meeting and clarified that the word referred to the meeting, not permission to behave extraordinarily.

It was resolved that:

  • The premises would remain closed pending the relevant assurances.
  • The photograph would not be included in the newsletter.
  • Members should stop contacting the local paper “to put them straight”.
  • The urn would be assessed separately.

Mrs Entwistle was thanked for securing an alternative meeting room at short notice. The deposit on the chairs remains under discussion.

Social subcommittee · 29 September 2026

The proposed variety afternoon received broad support. The following matters were recorded:

  • A magician may be booked, provided the Treasurer’s ledger is not used as a prop.
  • The final singalong should finish on a high note, where practicable.
  • Chicken in a basket was considered appropriate. The basket situation requires further investigation.
  • It was agreed that guests should leave wanting more, rather than waiting for a taxi at midnight.

Our proud heritage

Two districts. One committee.

Formed in 1968 following the historic merger of the Grimblewick Recreation Rooms and Clagworth Social Institute, the club has upheld a proud tradition of live entertainment, fellowship and orderly disagreement.

The order of the names was settled by a vote. The result continues to be respected by most members.
